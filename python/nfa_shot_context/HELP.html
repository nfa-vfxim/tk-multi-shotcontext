Shot Context

MAYA · VFXIM PIPELINE

Shot Context lets you check your animation in the cut. One click playblasts your shot and plays it between the shot before it and the shot after it, so you can judge continuity, timing and screen direction across the cuts.

Starting it

  1. Start Maya from ShotGrid Desktop and open your shot through ShotGrid > File Open. The tool only works inside a ShotGrid shot.
  2. Look through your shot camera in the viewport and set the timeline to your shot's frame range.
  3. Choose ShotGrid > Shot Context....

Your playblast is never submitted to ShotGrid. It's saved in your step's maya/previews/shot_context folder so artists on the neighbouring shots see it too. Only your three newest takes are kept.

What you see

By default three shots: the one before, yours, and the one after (change it with the Shots box). The picture shows each shot's number in the cut, e.g. SHOT 21/50. On the first shot of the film you get your shot and the next two; on the last shot, the two before and yours.

The order is the shot code, sequence by sequence, so the last shot of one sequence is followed by the first shot of the next. Omitted shots are skipped.

For each neighbouring shot the tool shows the first thing it finds:

PLAYBLASTThe newest playblast: either the newest one on ShotGrid (Review tool) or the newest one in the shot's Maya folders (Shot Context, Review tool, any movie in maya/previews), whichever is newer. Its name and age are shown in the corner.
FOOTAGENo playblast yet: the plate reference movie from 02_source, a published plate movie, or a JPG/PNG plate sequence.
NO FOOTAGE FOUNDNo footage either: a card for as long as the shot's duration on ShotGrid (cut duration, cut in/out, or the plate's frame count).
NO FOOTAGENo duration known at all: a card of 2 seconds.
ERRORA movie was found but couldn't be read. The reason is shown on the card.

Movies are read once into a local cache (%TEMP%\nfa_shot_context\cache), so they play frame accurately and open instantly the next time. Playback starts by itself as soon as everything is loaded.

Troubleshooting

  • "ShotGrid is not running in this Maya": Maya was started from the Start menu. Start it from ShotGrid Desktop.
  • "Shot Context only works inside a shot": you're in an asset or project context. Open your shot work file through ShotGrid > File Open.
  • Wrong camera in the playblast: click in the viewport with your shot camera before you start; the tool playblasts the active viewport.
  • "saved locally" in the status bar: the shot folder wasn't writable. You can still watch it, but your neighbours won't see it.
  • No sound / sound problems: set the environment variable NFA_SHOT_CONTEXT_NO_AUDIO=1 to play without sound.
  • The Script Editor logs what was chosen for each shot, starting with [NFA Shot Context].
